## **Information Still Required from the Official Syllabus**

The transcript establishes the names of courses, marks, grades, grade points, credits earned and semester performance. It does **not** establish the detailed curriculum needed for German Master's admission prerequisite assessment.

For a subsequent university-specific eligibility assessment, the following should be obtained from the official syllabus or university documentation:

- Detailed course descriptions
- Topics and units covered in each course
- Learning outcomes
- Mathematical topics and depth of coverage
- Theory versus practical components
- Weekly teaching hours
- Practical laboratory hours
- Assessment structure
- Independent study workload
- Programming language and tools used
- Specific algorithms and data structures taught
- Statistical methods covered
- Database concepts and SQL content
- Machine-learning algorithms covered
- Cloud computing and distributed systems topics
- Data engineering and Big Data topics
- Project requirements and scope

**Particularly important for German admissions:** your transcript alone cannot establish the exact amount of mathematics, statistics, theoretical computer science, algorithms, programming, and database coursework in the form of subject credits that individual German universities require.
